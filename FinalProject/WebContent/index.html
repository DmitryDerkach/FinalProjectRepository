Pharmacy

The final project of the dmdev servlets course (November 2021): an online drugstore without frameworks. Every request passes two filters before a servlet: EncodingFilter (UTF-8) and AuthorizationFilter (logged in? which role?). Who you are lives in the session.

browser→EncodingFilter→AuthorizationFilter→servlet→JSP

Log in with one click (demo accounts)

POST/login

The buttons send the same form as the login page. LoginServlet puts the user into the session and redirects by role.

GET/login · /registration

The pages themselves: admin@example.com / admin, anna@example.com / anna, or register a new customer.

Language and logout

POST/locale

LocaleServlet stores the language in the session; fmt:message takes the texts from translations_ru_RU.properties or translations.properties.

POST/logout

session.invalidate(): the user, the cart and the language are gone.
